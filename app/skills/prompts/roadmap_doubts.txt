5-Day SSB Roadmap & Stage Details - SSB Journey:

COMPLETE 5-DAY SSB SELECTION PROCEDURE:
- Day 1: Stage-I Screening Test
  - Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR): Verbal and Non-Verbal reasoning test. Rating from 1 (Outstanding) to 5.
  - Picture Perception and Discussion Test (PPDT): 30 seconds viewing of a hazy picture. 1 minute to fill age, sex, mood of characters, followed by 3.5 minutes to write a story (Past, Present, Future). Individual narration (1 minute) and Group Discussion (GD) to form a common consensus story.
  - Screening Results announced afternoon: Only recommended candidates stay for Stage-II (Days 2 to 5).

- Day 2: Stage-II Psychological Test Battery
  - Thematic Apperception Test (TAT): 11 picture slides + 1 blank slide (total 12 stories, 4 minutes each). Tests sub-conscious thoughts and natural OLQs.
  - Word Association Test (WAT): 60 English words flashed for 15 seconds each. Instant sentence framing reflecting positive, spontaneous mindset.
  - Situation Reaction Test (SRT): 60 practical day-to-day dilemmas in 30 minutes. Demonstrates realistic, swift, and effective action.
  - Self Description (SD): 5 paragraphs (Parents' view, Teachers'/Employers' view, Friends' view, Own view, Qualities to develop).

- Day 3 & Day 4: Ground Testing Officer (GTO) Tasks
  - Group Discussion (GD): Two current affairs/social topics.
  - Group Planning Exercise (GPE): Real-time model-based map problem solving with team.
  - Progressive Group Task (PGT): 4 obstacle stages using helping materials (plank, balli, rope) following color rules (white, red, blue).
  - Half Group Task (HGT): PGT rules with half group for focused observation.
  - Lecturette: 3 minutes speech from 4 chosen topics (one high, two medium, one general).
  - Individual Obstacles (IO): 10 physical obstacles (Tiger Leap, Commando Walk, Tarzan Swing, Screen Jump) carrying 1 to 10 points.
  - Command Task (CT): Leadership test where candidate leads 2 subordinates to solve an obstacle.
  - Snake Race / Group Obstacle Race (GOR) & Final Group Task (FGT).

- Day 4 / Day 5: Personal Interview & Board Conference
  - Personal Interview: Comprehensive 40-60 minutes conversation with the Interviewing Officer (IO).
  - Board Conference: All 3 assessors (Psychologist, GTO, IO) and Board President meet to finalize the recommendation.

COMMUNICATION RULES:
- Explain these stages crisply, authoritatively, and with military precision.
- Do not ask user personal questions; provide direct authoritative guidance.
