15 Officer Like Qualities (OLQ) & Interview Details - SSB Journey:

THE 15 OFFICER LIKE QUALITIES (OLQs) IN 4 FACTORS:
- Factor I: Planning & Organizing (Intellectual Abilities)
  1. Effective Intelligence: The practical capacity to cope with situations and tackle problems logically.
  2. Reasoning Ability: The power to grasp circumstances, analyze pros and cons, and arrive at rational conclusions.
  3. Organizing Ability: The ability to systematically arrange available tools and men to achieve a chosen goal.
  4. Power of Expression: The ability to present thoughts clearly, forcefully, and coherently.

- Factor II: Social Adjustment (Heart & Team Spirit)
  5. Social Adaptability: The ability to easily adjust with persons of all social backgrounds, ranks, and environments.
  6. Cooperation: Selfless willingness to work with colleagues towards a common objective, sacrificing personal ego.
  7. Sense of Responsibility: Dutifulness, moral discipline, and doing what is right even when unsupervised.

- Factor III: Social Effectiveness (Leadership Under Pressure)
  8. Initiative: Originating purposeful action without waiting to be told or prompted.
  9. Self Confidence: Faith in one's own capability to tackle difficult or unexpected situations successfully.
  10. Speed of Decision: Arriving at sound decisions quickly in real-time.
  11. Ability to Influence the Group: Inspiring and motivating group members to willingly adopt one's vision.
  12. Liveliness: Maintaining a cheerful, buoyant spirit in adversity and keeping team morale high.

- Factor IV: Dynamic (Courage & Grit)
  13. Determination: Sustained effort to achieve the objective despite fatigue, hurdles, or setbacks.
  14. Courage: Ability to face and overcome physical, mental, and moral danger without panic.
  15. Stamina: Physical endurance and mental toughness to withstand prolonged strain.

INTERVIEWING OFFICER (IO) PARAMETERS:
- The IO evaluates candidate background via the PIQ (Personal Information Questionnaire).
- Focus areas: Academic consistency, relations with parents/siblings/friends, sports, hobbies, general awareness, and defense knowledge.
- Deliver these details directly and conversationally without running an interactive interrogation.
