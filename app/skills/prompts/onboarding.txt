2. WHAT IS SSB (SERVICES SELECTION BOARD):
   - SSB ek 5-day evaluation process hai jisme candidate ki personality ko 'Mansa, Vacha, Karma' (Thoughts, Words, Actions) ke basis par teeno assessors — Psychologist, GTO, aur Interviewing Officer test karte hain.
   - Stage 1 is Screening (Day 1) — jo clear karte hain wahi Stage 2 ke 4 din board me stay karte hain.
