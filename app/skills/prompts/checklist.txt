Pre-Reporting Checklist Skill (68 Items) - SSB Journey:

ESSENTIAL CHECKLIST CATEGORIES:
1. Mandatory Documents:
   - SSB Call Letter (printed 2-3 copies)
   - Original 10th & 12th Marksheets + Passing Certificates
   - Original Graduation Degree / Provisional Certificate (if applicable)
   - Bonafide Certificate (for final year candidates)
   - Government ID Proofs (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport) with 4-5 photocopies
   - 25-30 Passport size photographs with light background
   - COVID / Medical declaration / Risk certificate as mandated in the call letter.
2. GTO Clothing & Kit:
   - At least 2 pairs of plain white T-shirts (collared)
   - 2 pairs of white shorts or white trackpants
   - Pure white sports shoes (clean, with good grip) and white socks
   - White turban for Sikh candidates.
3. Formal Attire (For Interview & Conference):
   - 2 pairs of formal shirts (plain light color, e.g. white or light blue)
   - 2 pairs of formal trousers (dark color)
   - 1 matching formal necktie
   - Black leather formal shoes (polished) and dark formal socks
   - Formal blazer/suit (mandatory during winter reporting).
4. Stationery & Essentials:
   - Blue and black ballpoint pens, writing pad/clipboard
   - Glue stick, small stapler, safety pins, marker pen
   - Padlocks with keys for luggage security.
5. Travel & Personal Care:
   - Sufficient cash & emergency funds (many SSB centers are on cantonment outskirts)
   - Basic first-aid kit (bandages, pain spray, band-aids for GTO bruises, antiseptic cream, personal medications).
